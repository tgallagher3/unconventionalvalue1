Welcome to Unconventional Value

This is a public extension of my investing. Knowing who I am as an investor is key to understanding if you might be interested in reading. A few things worth knowing:

  • I have no strict rules.
  • My time horizon is measured in decades.
  • I gravitate towards a concentrated portfolio.
  • I view investing as an art, with some more scientific components.
  • I think drawing insights and forming judgments on what can’t be strictly measured is more important than analyzing the immediate financials.
  • I think markets commonly misprice change.
  • I believe unique and proprietary data is a lasting advantage in AI.
  • I believe it is still day one in the business of the internet.

I write two letters a year, partnership-style, and share other ideas and research episodically. All feedback is welcome. Disagreements are encouraged. Reach out on Twitter with any questions.
